01 — The Studio

Built for the details others miss.

Masters of Detailing is a dedicated car care and surface-protection studio near Karahiya Bridge, Rewa. The space was purpose-built for detailing — a hex-lit inspection bay that shows every swirl and scratch, a separate wash area, and an enclosed paint booth for body shop work.

Our focus is simple: careful preparation, quality products, and a finish you can see in the reflection.

  • /01
    Protection first

    PPF, ceramic and graphene coatings to keep paint looking new for longer.

  • /02
    Correct, then protect

    Paint correction to remove swirl marks and light scratches before any coating goes on.

  • /03
    One roof

    Detailing, films, denting & painting, accidental repairs and insurance claims.
